Frequently Asked Questions
How do I add a new employee? +
Go to the Employees page and click the "Add Employee" button in the top right. Fill in the required fields like name, email, department, and role. The employee will be added to the system immediately.
How do I approve a leave request? +
Navigate to Leave Approval from the sidebar. You'll see all pending requests. Click the green ✓ Approve button or red ✕ Reject button next to each request. You can also approve multiple requests at once.
How do I generate and download reports? +
Go to Reports from the sidebar. Choose your report type (Attendance, Leave, or Employee), select the date range, and click Generate. You can download as PDF or Excel, or email it directly.
Can I edit employee information? +
Yes! Open the Employee Profile by clicking View on any employee in the list. Click the Edit button to enable editing mode, make changes, then click Save Changes in the header.
How are notifications sent? +
Notifications appear in the Notifications page and also as bell alerts in the header. You can configure email notifications and daily digests in Settings → Notification Preferences.
